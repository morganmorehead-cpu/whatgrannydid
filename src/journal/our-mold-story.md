We’re not living in our home right now. Here’s why.

A few weeks ago, I noticed mold around one of our air vents. Something told me not to ignore it. We called an HVAC specialist who focuses on mold, and when he got up into the attic, he found the inside of our air ducts was riddled with mold because the builder cut corners. Every time our air ran, it was moving through our whole house.

## The signs we missed

Looking back, the signs were there. I was having high histamine reactions. My kids struggled with sleep. None of us ever felt rested, and everyone had gut issues. I never connected the dots to our house.

## What we’ve done so far

- Replaced all of the ducts, vents, and our AC unit
- Let go of most of what we owned
- Fogged the house with SuperStratum
- Deep cleaned twice
- Repainted and replaced carpet with wood floors

We’re still living elsewhere while we finish. And here’s what’s giving me hope: since we’ve been out of the house, we’ve noticed a real difference in our kids’ behavior.

## What I want you to know

1. **Don’t ignore the signs,** in your home or your family.
2. **Find the source.** Cleaning and fogging can’t fix mold that’s still growing in your ducts or walls.
3. **Hire someone who specializes in mold.**
4. **Don’t guess, test.** Ask about clearance testing before moving back in.

> Things can be replaced. My family can’t.

It’s been hard, and losing so much stung. But God has been faithful through every step. I’ll keep sharing as we rebuild and recover.
