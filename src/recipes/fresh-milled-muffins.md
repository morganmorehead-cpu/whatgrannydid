---
title: "Fresh-milled muffins"
description: "Soft, simple muffins sweetened with honey or Sucanat, with a sourdough option."
date: 2026-10-18
category: "Bread and baking"
prepTime: "15 minutes"
cookTime: "20 minutes"
servings: "12 muffins"
image: ""
ingredients:
  - "2 cups fresh-milled soft white wheat flour"
  - "2 tsp aluminum-free baking powder"
  - "½ tsp salt"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "½ cup melted butter"
  - "½ cup honey or Sucanat"
  - "¾ cup raw milk"
  - "1 cup blueberries, or 2 mashed bananas"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 375°F and butter or line a muffin tin."
  - "Whisk the dry ingredients in a bowl."
  - "Stir in the wet ingredients until just combined, then fold in the fruit."
  - "Let the batter rest 10 minutes, since fresh-milled flour soaks up liquid."
  - "Bake 18 to 22 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean."
---
**Sourdough version:** Stir in 1 cup of sourdough discard, reduce the flour to 1½ cups and the milk to ¼ cup, and add ½ tsp baking soda.

Muffins keep 3 days on the counter, 5 in the fridge, or 3 months frozen.
