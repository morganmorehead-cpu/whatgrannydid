---
title: "Fresh-milled sausage balls"
description: "Our batch-cook breakfast and snack. Make a double batch and freeze the rest."
date: 2026-10-18
category: "Breakfast"
prepTime: "15 minutes"
cookTime: "20 minutes"
servings: "About 30 balls"
image: ""
ingredients:
  - "1 lb pastured pork sausage"
  - "2 cups shredded raw cheddar"
  - "1½ cups fresh-milled soft white wheat flour"
  - "2 tsp aluminum-free baking powder"
  - "½ tsp salt"
  - "4 tbsp melted butter"
  - "¼ to ⅓ cup raw milk"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 375°F and line a sheet pan with parchment."
  - "Mix everything by hand until combined."
  - "Roll into 1-inch balls and space them on the pan."
  - "Bake 18 to 22 minutes, until golden and cooked through to 160°F."
---
Refrigerate up to 4 days or freeze up to 3 months. Reheat at 350°F for about 10 minutes.
